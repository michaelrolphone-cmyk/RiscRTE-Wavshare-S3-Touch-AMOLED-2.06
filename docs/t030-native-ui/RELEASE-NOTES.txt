Waveshare 0.1.44 native-resolution Springboard (prerelease)

Built only over the exact published 0.1.43 catalog-acquisition factory. The previous
catalog fix is retained. The selected source pins and assembly report identify all
changed components; every other stored application/provider remains byte-for-byte.
The native-resolution work applies to Springboard; it does not migrate every app or
Quick Controls to the native raster path. The known-ineligible generic grid retains
compact bitmap text and fixed controls, with corrected native icons/contact. Renderer fixtures and full-store admission
are separate checks; neither proves complete app/device acceptance.
The eligible Springboard honeycomb renders original licensed font/icon outlines
directly at capability-native dimensions and uses elapsed-time-correct input waits. The existing Home receives
its missing retained-wake permission so its deep-wake branch is available; its
ELF and genuine cold-start animation remain unchanged. The adapter restores the
current brightness/torch level once after its first completed frame when supported.
The existing 40 ms controller interval remains, at most 25 requested frames/s.
Software validation is not a hardware speed measurement or proof of a hardware fix.
Hardware validation of this candidate is pending. The device trace uses a bounded
64-record history ring with all 64 cumulative counters preserved; capture perf
soon after a gesture while remaining in Springboard. See the included decoder
and timing guide for missing/overwritten data and units.

The full 16 MiB factory BIN is flashed at offset 0 and ERASES SAVED INTERNAL DATA.
For preservation, use the separate kit only with a fresh full-device readback of a
confirmed exact 0.1.43 installation. Never flash its baseline.bin or candidate.bin.
Stage the inactive firmware/store/state, verify all readbacks, then write OTA selection
LAST. Never erase_flash. Follow the included preserving instructions and stop on error.
The kit does not connect to a device. SD contents are unchanged. T023 provisioning is excluded.

Evidence covers exact source custody, native ELF/BIN/profile/vendor proof, complete
62-ELF source-bound admission in normal and sanitized modes, both source banks,
selection interruption cuts, corrupt-input rejection, and packaged planner/readback checks.
